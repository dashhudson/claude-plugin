# Dash Social for Claude

Connect Claude to your Dash Social account for social media analytics, competitive intelligence, community insights, content management, and scheduling.

## Requirements

- A Dash Social account with access to the brands you want to use
- Claude Code or Claude Cowork

## Connect

Install the Dash Social plugin from Claude's plugin directory. When Claude asks you to connect your Dash Social account, follow the sign-in flow and approve access. Claude will only have access to the brands and capabilities available to your account.

In Claude Code, you can open `/mcp` to view the connection or reconnect your account.

## What you can do

- Compare social performance across brands, channels, and date ranges.
- Analyze top-performing owned, earned, and competitor content.
- Summarize community messages, sentiment, and emerging themes.
- Identify content-calendar gaps and best times to post.
- Create draft scheduled posts using media from your Dash Social library.
